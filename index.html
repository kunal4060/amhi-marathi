आमच्याबद्दल · About us

Maharashtra is far. Home isn't.

We're the Marathi cultural association of VIT-AP — students from every corner of Maharashtra (and friends who just love the culture) keeping our festivals, our language and our noise alive, a thousand kilometres from home.

ढोल वाजला की VIT-AP ला कळतं — आम्ही आलोय.

When the dhol plays, the whole campus knows we're here. Our dhol-tasha pathak leads every celebration, our Ganeshotsav is the flagship week of the year, and between festivals we're the people who'll argue about the best vada pav with complete seriousness.

New in Amaravati and missing home? You'll find your district, your food, and your people here. Not Marathi? Even better — come for the dhol, stay for the puran poli.
